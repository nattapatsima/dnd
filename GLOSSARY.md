# Glossary

**Build**
A character's fixed numbers and choices at one level: ability scores, attacks, daily uses, features, DM rulings. It is checked against the rules and against the character's written sheet, and it changes only on a level-up or a ruling. The player never edits it at the table.

**Table state**
Everything the player changes during play: hit points, spent uses and hit dice, death saves, toggles, ammunition, gear text, coins, notes. It is saved on the device under the character's own name and survives Build updates.

**Character page**
One character's sheet at its own address, with its own Home Screen app and its own saved Table state. The Phantom Sniper, Dyme and the Autognome Bard are Character pages of one app.

**Written sheet**
The readable record of a Build (`Characters/<name>.md` in the DnD workspace). It is the source of truth: every number a Character page shows must match it.

**DM ruling**
A table decision that overrides or settles the rules for one character, such as a custom weapon or a disallowed trait. A Build lists its DM rulings so they're on hand at the table.

**Effect chip**
A Play-tab toggle for a per-turn feature (Steady Aim, Lengthened Strike, Burst of Speed). While it is on, the numbers it affects (to-hit, reach, speed) show the changed values. It never spends a daily use, and a Long rest turns it off.

**Spells tab**
A full caster's spellbook (a Build with spell slots): spellcasting line on top, then spells grouped by level, each level header showing its slot dots. Builds without slots keep spells on a combined Features and spells tab.

**Worked number**
A number in a Build's text that the sheet calculates from the Build (spell save DC, spell attack, an ability modifier, proficiency) instead of storing it typed, so it can't drift after a level-up.
